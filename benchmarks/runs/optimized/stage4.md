# Regulatory Impact Analysis — Banking Control Law

## 1. Executive Summary

The Banking Control Law (SAMA M/5) imposes 39 regulatory obligations, primarily of high criticality, across seven core requirement groups. It mandates comprehensive controls over licensing, capital, credit, governance, and reporting. The regulation's impact is significant, with a strong focus on preventive and ongoing operational controls.

## 2. Requirement Overview

The regulation is structured into seven key requirement groups: Licensing and Authorization, Capital/Reserves/Liquidity, Credit/Exposure Limits, Prohibited Activities/Investments, Governance/Personnel, Reporting/Disclosure, and Prior Approvals/Notifications. These groups encompass 39 specific obligations, with a predominant focus on high-criticality requirements. The obligations are categorized as Preventive, Governance, and Reporting types.

## 6. Architectural & Operational Implications

The high proportion of Ongoing_Control and Preventive obligations necessitates robust, embedded operational processes and controls. The significant Governance and Prior Approval requirements will demand enhanced oversight structures and formalized decision-making workflows. The reporting obligations will require reliable data capture and management systems to ensure timely and accurate disclosures.
